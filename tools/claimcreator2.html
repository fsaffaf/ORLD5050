Main Claim Builder

Build a focused, debatable claim connected to your graduate program. Start with a broad area of interest, identify a specific organizational issue, take a position, and explain three reasons that support your position.

Step 1. Choose Your Degree

Select your graduate program. The examples in the next steps will be tailored to your selection.

Step 2. Choose an Area of Interest

Which part of your field interests you most? Choose a broad topic that you would like to learn more about.

Step 3. Identify an Organizational Issue

What problem, challenge, or decision within this area should organizations address? Narrow your topic to an issue that could be examined using research and evidence.

Step 4. Choose Your Position

What do you think organizations should do about this issue? Choose a position that someone could reasonably disagree with.

Step 5. Explain Three Reasons

Why should organizations take this position? Write three different reasons. Each reason should explain a potential benefit, outcome, or improvement that you could support with evidence.

Try to make each reason distinct. For example, do not list three different ways the same outcome could occur. Each reason could become a supporting point in your essay.

Before You Use Your Claim

The builder creates a starting point, not a finished argument. Review your sentence and make sure it expresses a position you can defend.

  • Debatable: A reasonable person could disagree with your position.
  • Focused: Your claim identifies a specific issue within a clear area of interest.
  • Evidence-based: You can find credible evidence to support your position and reasons.
  • Logical: Your three reasons genuinely explain why the organization should take this position.
