Before You Use Your Claim

The builder creates a starting point, not a finished argument. Review your sentence and make sure it expresses a position you can defend.

  • Debatable: A reasonable person could disagree with your position.
  • Focused: The setting and application are specific enough for your assignment.
  • Evidence-based: You can find credible evidence to support your position and reasons.
  • Logical: Your three reasons genuinely support the position you selected.
