Main Claim Builder

Choose your graduate program, then build a focused main claim by selecting or writing an action, three distinct reasons, and the person or group responsible for taking action. Each step offers options tailored to your program.

1Pick your program

Choose the graduate program you are enrolled in. The remaining options will be based on your selection.

2Pick your what

What specific action do you think should happen? Choose an option related to your program, or select “Type my own answer…” to enter your own action.

I think that [WHO] should [WHAT], because [WHY 1], [WHY 2], and [WHY 3].

3Pick your why

Choose three different reasons that explain why your recommendation may be helpful. Each dropdown includes options related to your program. To write your own reason, select “Type my own answer…” for that reason.

4Pick your who

Who should be responsible for taking the action? Choose a group or organization related to your program, or select “Type my own answer…” to enter your own.

Your completed main claim

This is a starting point for your argument. You will need credible evidence to support the recommendation and explain why the reasons justify the proposed action.
