Before you use this claim: Make sure your topic is specific enough to investigate. You should be able to find credible evidence about the problem and the proposed action. Avoid claims that require confidential company records, private employee information, or access to an entire organization's internal systems. You can narrow a broad topic to one industry, workplace process, group, or measurable outcome.
