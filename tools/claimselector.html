Main Claim Builder

Choose your graduate program, then build a focused main claim by selecting or writing an action, three distinct reasons, and the person or group responsible for taking action. Each step offers options tailored to your program.

1Select your Program

Choose the graduate program you are enrolled in. The remaining options will be based on your selection.

2Select your Main Claim

Select an option from the drop-down menu below, or select “Type my own answer…” to enter your own answer.

3Pick Three Reasons Why

Choose three different reasons that explain why your main claim is true. Select an option from the drop-down menu below, or select “Type my own answer…” to enter your own answer.

4Who is Responsible?

Who should be responsible for taking the action? Select an option from the drop-down menu below, or select “Type my own answer…” to enter your own answer.

Your completed main claim

This is a starting point for your argument. You will need credible evidence to support the recommendation and explain why the reasons justify the proposed action.
