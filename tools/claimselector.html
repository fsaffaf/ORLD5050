Main Claim Builder

Build a focused, debatable claim about applied analytics and decision-making. Choose a position, identify a setting, narrow the topic to a business application, and select three supporting reasons.

Part 1. Choose Your Position

What do you think organizations should do? Choose a position that someone could reasonably disagree with.

Part 2. Choose Your Industry or Setting

Where would this issue occur? Choose an organizational setting that gives your essay a clear focus.

Part 3. Narrow Your Business Application

What specific organizational decision or process should your claim address? Choose an application that fits your selected setting.

Tip: A focused application makes it easier to find relevant evidence and explain the practical consequences of your position.

Part 4. Select Three Supporting Reasons

Why should an organization take this position? Choose three different reasons that you could support with evidence.

Choose reasons that are meaningfully different from one another. Each reason could become a supporting point in your essay.

Before You Use Your Claim

The builder creates a starting point, not a finished argument. Review your sentence and make sure it expresses a position you can defend.

  • Debatable: A reasonable person could disagree with your position.
  • Focused: The setting and business application are specific enough for your assignment.
  • Evidence-based: You can find credible evidence to support your position and reasons.
  • Logical: Your three reasons genuinely support the position you selected.
