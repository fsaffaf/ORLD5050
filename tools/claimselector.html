Main Claim Builder

Choose your graduate program and complete the sentence to develop a focused claim about a workplace problem, a proposed action, and why that action matters.

1. Which graduate program are you in?
2. WHO should take action?

Identify the group or role that has the ability to address the problem.

3. DO WHAT should they do?

Choose a specific action rather than a vague goal.

4. WHY should they do it?

Select the benefit or problem that explains why the action matters.

5. Review your sentence
Sentence structure:
I think that [WHO] should [DO WHAT], because [WHY].
Your completed main claim

This is a starting point for your argument. You will need credible evidence to support both the proposed action and the reason it matters.
Before you use your claim: Make sure the topic is specific enough to investigate using credible, accessible evidence. Avoid topics that require confidential company records or private employee information. If the topic is too broad, narrow it to a particular workplace process, population, setting, or measurable outcome.
