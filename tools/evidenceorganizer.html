Evidence Organizer

Use this tool to organize your main claim, subclaims, and supporting evidence from the articles you select for your research paper.

Claims

Enter your main claim and the three subclaims that make up your argument.

Article Citation

Enter the first author's last name and the year the article was published. Answer the questions below so the organizer can automatically format the in-text citation for your quotations and paraphrases.

The organizer will automatically format your citation according to the number of authors.

Evidence and Argument Connections

Use this section to identify evidence from your article and explain how it supports your argument. Start by selecting the subclaim you want to support. Then find a relevant quotation, explain what the quotation communicates in your own words, and describe why the evidence supports your selected subclaim. Remember: A quotation alone does not explain why your argument is correct. You must explain how the evidence supports the specific subclaim you selected.

Add as many pieces of evidence as you need. You do not need to find a specific number of quotations in every article.
