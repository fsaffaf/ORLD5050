Argument and Article Overview

Entering your main claim and the three subclaims that make up your argument. Then record basic information about the article you are reviewing.

Evidence and Evaluation

Use this section to identify evidence from an article and connect it to your subclaim(s).
Identify facts, statistics, or quotations from experts in the article that could support your argument. Your direct quotation should include at least one of these types of information. Connect each quotation to the specific subclaim it supports. Remember: A quotation alone does not explain why your argument is correct. You must explain how the evidence supports the specific subclaim you select.

Add as many pieces of evidence as you need. You do not need to find a specific number of quotations in every article.
