How to Use PhilMaps

Step-by-Step Guide

Getting Started

Create an argument map by organizing your main claim, supporting reasons, objections, and responses.

Open PhilMaps →

What is an argument map?

An argument map is a visual diagram that shows your main claim, the reasons supporting it, and possible objections. It helps you organize your reasoning and evaluate whether your argument is logical and well supported.

Step 1: Open the website

  1. Go to PhilMaps .
  2. Wait for the editor to load in your browser.
  3. If the editor does not load, refresh the page and try again.

Step 2: Add your main claim

Your main claim is the overall position you want to argue for or defend.

  1. Locate the option for adding a claim or statement.
  2. Enter your main claim as a complete sentence.
  3. Make sure your claim expresses a position someone could reasonably agree or disagree with.
Example

A technology company should require employees to complete cybersecurity training every year.

Step 3: Add supporting reasons

Supporting reasons explain why someone should accept your main claim.

  1. Select your main claim.
  2. Use the available options to add a supporting reason.
  3. Enter one clear reason at a time.
  4. Repeat to add other reasons that support your claim.
Examples
  • Annual training can help employees recognize phishing attempts.
  • Regular practice can reinforce secure workplace habits.

Step 4: Add objections and responses

An objection is a reason someone might disagree with your claim or challenge one of your supporting reasons.

  1. Select the claim or reason you want to challenge.
  2. Use the available option to add an objection or opposing claim.
  3. Write the objection as a complete sentence.
  4. If appropriate, add a response explaining how you would address the objection.
Example objection

Annual cybersecurity training takes employees away from their regular work.

Example response

Short training sessions can reduce disruption while helping employees recognize security risks.

Step 5: Review your argument map

Before saving your work, check each of the following:

  • ☐ My main claim clearly states my position.
  • ☐ Each supporting reason explains why my claim is reasonable.
  • ☐ I have considered relevant objections.
  • ☐ My responses address the objections I included.
  • ☐ My statements are clear, specific, and logically connected.

Step 6: Save and submit your work

  1. Look for the File menu or the available save options.
  2. Save an editable copy of your argument map to your computer or a supported cloud location.
  3. If your assignment requires a PDF, use the available export or print option and select PDF as the destination if offered.
  4. Open your saved file to make sure it displays correctly.
  5. Submit the file in the format required by your instructor.
Important

Keep an editable copy of your map so you can make changes later. Available saving and export options may vary depending on the editor.
